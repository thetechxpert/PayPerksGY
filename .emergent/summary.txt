<original_problem_statement>
Build a native mobile app MVP called PayPerks GY for Guyana to incentivize debit card and digital payment usage through merchant rewards and perks. The platform must support three roles: User (Consumer), Merchant (Business Partner), and Admin. The MVP should function without any bank, Revolut, or payment gateway integrations. Reward validation will be done via QR code redemption, receipt upload, and merchant confirmation.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS**

**User Roles &amp; Permissions**
- **User (Consumer):** Register/login, create a profile, browse/redeem offers (QR/receipt), view redemption history, and view points balance.
- **Merchant:** Register/login, complete a profile (requires Admin approval), create/edit/deactivate offers, view/confirm redemptions, and view simple analytics.
- **Admin:** Approve/reject merchants, activate/deactivate offers, view platform metrics, suspend users/merchants, and review receipt-based redemptions.

**Data Models**
- **Users:** (id, role, name, email, phone, location, preferred_card_type, created_at, status)
- **Merchants:** (id, role, business_name, category, location, logo_url, contact_info, approved_bool, rejected_reason, created_at, status)
- **Offers:** (id, merchant_id, title, description, reward_type, reward_value, start_date, end_date, redemption_rules, active_bool, created_at)
- **Redemptions:** (id, user_id, offer_id, merchant_id, method, proof_url, status, rejection_reason, redeemed_at, created_at)
- **PointsLedger:** (id, user_id, delta_points, reason, ref_type, ref_id, created_at)

**Core Screens**
- **Public/Auth:** Landing, Login, Register
- **User App:** Offers Feed, Offer Detail, Redeem Flow (QR scan + receipt upload), Rewards/Points Wallet, My Redemptions, Profile
- **Merchant Portal:** Dashboard, Profile, Create/Edit Offer, Redemptions List, Basic Analytics
- **Admin Panel:** Dashboard, Merchant Approvals, Offer Management, Redemptions Review, User/Merchant Management

**Redemption &amp; Points Logic**
- QR redemptions are instantly approved.
- Receipt uploads are pending until Admin/Merchant approval.
- Points are awarded based on the offer's .
- All point changes are tracked in .

**UX &amp; Technical Requirements**
- Mobile-first design with role-based access.
- Image uploads for logos and receipts.
- Basic search and filter on offers.
- Prevent duplicate redemptions (e.g., once per day per user per offer).
</original_problem_statement>
